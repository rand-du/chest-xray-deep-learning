# chest-xray-deep-learning
Comparative Analysis of Deep Learning Models and Metaheuristic Optimization for Chest X-ray Classification.


## Overview

Medical imaging offers an opportunity to explore how computational intelligence can support the analysis of complex visual patterns.

This research investigates deep learning approaches for chest X-ray classification, with a focus on evaluating different CNN architectures and exploring the role of metaheuristic optimization in improving model performance.

Rather than focusing on a single architecture, the study adopts a comparative experimental approach to examine model performance, optimization strategies, and generalization.

## Research Focus

**Five CNN Architectures**

- EfficientNetB0
- ResNet50
- DenseNet121
- MobileNetV3Large
- InceptionV3

**Metaheuristic Optimization**

Two optimization approaches will be investigated to explore their impact on model performance and hyperparameter selection.

**Comparative Evaluation**

Baseline and optimized models will be assessed using consistent evaluation protocols, quantitative metrics, and visual analysis.

## Research Pipeline

`Dataset → Preprocessing → CNN Models → Optimization → Evaluation → Comparison`

## Experimental Analysis

The study will examine:

- Classification performance and model behavior.
- Training and validation dynamics.
- The effect of optimization on model performance.
- Comparative performance across architectures.
- Generalization across data sources, if supported by the final dataset design.
